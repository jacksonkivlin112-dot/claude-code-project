## Description

Take control of your money one paycheck at a time. 💚

The 2027 Paycheck Budget Planner is a Google Sheets template that shows exactly which bills each paycheck needs to cover, what you're saving, and what's left to spend, before you spend it. Log your purchases as you go and your dashboard, charts and savings goals update automatically. No formulas to write and no spreadsheet skills needed.

✦ WHAT'S INCLUDED ✦

• Start Here: a quick 5-step guide plus your settings (name, pay frequency, first payday, currency)
• Dashboard: total income, expenses, savings and savings rate, a month-by-month table, an income vs. expenses bar chart and a spending-by-category pie chart
• Paycheck Planner: every 2027 payday filled in for you (weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly or monthly). Each paycheck shows the bills due before your next payday and your "left to spend", in green when you're ahead and red when you're over
• Bills: your recurring bills with due day, category, autopay and a paid checkbox, plus paid/unpaid totals
• Expenses: a 500-row transaction log with category dropdowns
• Categories: 12 ready-made categories (add your own) with monthly budgets and budget vs. actual for any month you pick
• Savings Goals: up to 5 goals with progress bars

✦ FEATURES ✦

• Works with weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly and monthly pay
• Bills are matched to the right paycheck automatically
• Any currency: set your symbol once
• Easy dropdowns for categories, months and pay frequency
• Calm sage and cream design that's easy on the eyes
• Comes with one sample month so you can see how it works, plus instructions to clear it
• Works on computer, tablet and phone with the free Google Sheets app
• Can be downloaded to Microsoft Excel from Google Sheets

✦ HOW IT WORKS ✦

1. Purchase and download the PDF from Etsy (You > Purchases)
2. Click the link inside and choose "Make a copy". The planner is saved to your own Google Drive
3. Enter your settings, add your bills and start planning
